TheComputeClub

Short animated explainers on computer science, maths and engineering.

About

TheComputeClub is an independent educational channel run by one creator, Aaditya Kaushik. It publishes one short animated video a day explaining a single STEM idea, such as an algorithm, a physics principle or a piece of how computers work.

Where to watch

YouTube: youtube.com/@TheComputeClub

How the videos are made

Videos are produced by a personal automation tool. It writes and fact-checks a short script, renders the animation with the open-source Manim library, adds narration and captions, and uploads the finished video to the creator's own YouTube channel. The tool is used only by the creator and is not offered to anyone else. Its source code is public on GitHub.
